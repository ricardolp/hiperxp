Perfect World Hero Saga, also known simply as Hero Saga, is a system within the game designed to tell a story, and contains sixteen different Sagas for sixteen heroes belonging to each of the classes. This system activates upon reaching level 80 with cultivation of Aware of Vacuity.

## Activating Sagas

To activate a Saga, players must go to the Quests menu in the bottom right of their game window, and select the fourth option, Perfect World Hero Saga. If this is their first time opening this window, a little poem will display. Otherwise, or if they proceed past the poem, they will see an interface with three heroes in portrait, along with their names above them. To the left and right of the three are one or more arrows allowing players to scroll between races.

## Requirements

Players must have an all-time highest level of 80 and a cultivation level of Aware of Vacuity to interact with the Hero Saga system. Some Sagas will additionally require that the player's active level be level 80, either at the start of the Saga or partway through. It is recommended for the sake of efficiency of experience to wait for level 100 to participate, as the experience rewards scale with level until level 100. All quest rewards listed are the amounts received at level 100, and quest rewards will be lower at lower levels.

## Claiming Saga Rewards

Each Hero Saga will yield a title which gives +100 HP and a pet egg which may be placed in the Codex. To receive these, players must click the glowing chest in the bottom-right of the Saga interface for the specific Hero's Saga for which they have completed (rewards may only be received once).

##### Reversal of Time

This quest will appear automatically once players have completed "Entering Memory."

Watch the cutscene - if the cutscene is skipped, the player will have to wait for the quest to run its timer out, at which point they will be transported to (431, 878) while having their screen become grayscale. The player will be transformed into Blademaster General Chung, during which they will not be able to use most items, any class skills, or any genie skills. They will instead be limited to the three skills in the bar in the center of the screen - which is able to be dragged to anywhere they wish. This transformation will last until the completion of "Roast Fish" in Chapter 1. These skills are as follows:

Tiger Maw

- A single focused strike that causes the enemy to suffer greater physical damage.
- Cooldown: 1 second

Heaven's Flame

- Summon a blazing dragon to blast all enemies within 12 meters of your opponent, dealing base physical damage and increases the damage they take for the next 6 seconds.
- Cooldown: 5 seconds

Speed of the Moon

- Call upon the moon to greatly increase your movement speed, lasts for 12 seconds.
- Cooldown: 13 seconds

Rewards: 144 000 Experience and 28 000 Spirit

##### A note to those who have been estranged or disowned:

Understand that the end message of the saga is intended for typical people. In the US, according to a recent survey, fewer than 5% of the population has either been estranged or disowned, either by their own initialization or that of their parents. No matter the circumstances of this estrangement or disownment, understand that we are the ones who understand the pain of having been torn asunder from our parents the most. It is important for these individuals to note that we have no obligation to our parents, and that love which is conditional on the parents' part is no love at all. If someone loves you, their love will not depend on who you are, and will be unwavering, and unjudging of who you are. It will not worship a false image of you but will be dedicated to you as a person.
